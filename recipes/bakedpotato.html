Baked Potato

Description

The perfect baked potato can be enjoyed on its own or as a side dish.

Ingredients:

  • Russet Potato
  • Butter
  • Oil + Salt (for the skin)
  • Optional Toppings:
    • Sour Cream
    • Shredded Cheese
    • Steamed/Microwaved Brocolli
    • Bacon

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 F
  2. While oven preheats prick clean potatoes with a fork, brush/roll/spray oil on outside of potatoes and sprinkle/roll on kosher salt
  3. Place potatoes directly onto oven rack
  4. Bake for 40-60+ mins until they reach 200+ degrees F, measured with a food thermometer
  5. Directly out of the oven cut each potato open long ways from one end to the other and put butter inside
  6. Use a spoon to carefully mix the butter in with the insides of the potato, keeping the skin intact
  7. Add optional toppings
