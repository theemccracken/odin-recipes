Pinto Beans

Description:

Perfectly cooked pinto beans are all about the timing.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound dry pinto beans
  • 6-8 cups of water
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Soak beans overnight/8 hours (no more than 12) in 8-10 cups of water, with a few tablespoons of salt is fine but not required
  2. Rinse dry beans
  3. Put beans in pot with at least 6 cups of water or more to add 1 inch of water over the top of the beans
  4. Add a heavy amount of salt
  5. Bring to a heavy boil for 10 mins
  6. After 10 mins reduce to a very light boil and cover for 1.5-2.5 hours
