Peanut Butter and Jelly

All Recipes (Home)

Description:

The peanut butter and jelly sandwich is a versatile and simple way to enjoy bread.

Ingredients:

  • 2 slices of bread per sandwich
  • Peanut butter, nut butter, alternatives
  • Jelly, jam, or preserves

Instructions:

  1. Take out two slices of bread and place them on a plate, seperated
  2. Spread your choice of nut butter on the top side of one slice
  3. Spread your choice of fruit spread on the top side of the second slice
  4. Put both topped sides together to enjoy a peanut butter and jelly sandwich.
